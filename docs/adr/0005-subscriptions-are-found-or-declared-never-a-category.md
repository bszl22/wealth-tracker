# Subscriptions are found or declared on rules, never a Category

A Subscription is a view across Categories, so the tracker refuses a Category named Subscriptions, from the rule form, a Suggested Rule or the rules file. Spending is in a Category for what it is for, such as Entertainment › Streaming, and whether it is a Subscription is said separately. Most Subscriptions are still found from repeating charges. Detection alone misses yearly ones for two years and monthly ones for their first months, so a Categorisation Rule can declare that it catches a Subscription, with its interval. A rule can also declare that it catches none, which keeps regular payments like rent out of the Subscriptions total.

## Considered Options

- A Subscriptions Category whose Transactions always show as Subscriptions. Easy to set up, but every Transaction has one Category, so Netflix could not be both Streaming and a Subscription, and the Spending breakdown would mix "what it's for" with "how often it charges".
- Detection only. Needs no marks, but a yearly Subscription stays invisible until its second charge, and rent counts as a Subscription with no way out.
